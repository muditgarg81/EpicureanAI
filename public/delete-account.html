Delete Account & Data

If you would like to permanently delete your Epicurean Modern Kitchen account and all associated data, you can do so easily.

Request Data Deletion

Alternative In-App Method

You can also instantly delete your account directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Epicurean app.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Tap on the Settings icon.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.

Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your saved recipes, meal plans, and flavor profiles will be permanently erased.
