Saffron Infused

Moroccan Spiced Lamb Roast

schedule 45 min
local_fire_department 640 cal
star Medium

Ingredients (4 Servings)

auto_awesome AI DIET TIP

Substitute lamb with chickpeas for a high-protein vegan alternative that pairs perfectly with the saffron marinade.

Instructions

01

Marinate the Lamb

Combine the saffron-infused oil, crushed garlic, and chili flakes in a small ceramic bowl. Rub the mixture thoroughly into the lamb shoulder, ensuring even coverage. Let it rest at room temperature for 20 minutes to allow the spices to penetrate the grain.

02

Searing Process

Preheat a heavy-based cast iron skillet over high heat. Sear the lamb on all sides until a deep, caramelized golden-brown crust forms. This Maillard reaction is critical for unlocking the epicurean depth of the Moroccan spices.

03

The Slow Roast

Transfer the skillet to a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F). Roast for 25 minutes for medium-rare. The internal temperature should reach a precise 55°C. Use the AI Coach timer for real-time tracking based on your oven's specific calibration.

04

Resting & Garnish

Allow the meat to rest for 10 minutes under loose foil. Slice against the grain and finish with a generous dollop of Greek yogurt, a shower of fresh pomegranate arils, and a final drizzle of premium olive oil.
