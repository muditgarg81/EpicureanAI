How do I enable voice control?
expand_more

To activate hands-free cooking, tap the microphone icon in the top right corner of any active recipe. You can also say "Hey Epicurean" to wake the coach. Ensure you've granted microphone permissions in your device settings for a seamless experience.

Can the coach adapt to my cooking speed?
expand_more

Yes! Our AI Coach uses "Adaptive Pace" technology. By monitoring your task completion or voice feedback (e.g., "Ready for the next step"), it learns your prep speed. You can manually adjust the tempo in Settings > AI Preferences > Pacing Mode.

edit_note
What should I do if the coach misinterprets an ingredient?
expand_more

If the scanner or voice input misidentifies an item, simply tap the ingredient name on your screen to open the Manual Correction menu. You can also say "Wait, that's not cumin, it's coriander" to trigger an instant smart-update.

timer
How to use multi-timer mode?
expand_more

In complex recipes, the AI Coach automatically spawns multiple timers for simultaneous tasks. You can view all active counts by swiping up from the bottom drawer. To add a manual timer, say "Start a 5-minute timer for the garnish."
