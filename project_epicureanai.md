# Project: Epicurean AI

## Overview
Epicurean AI is a premium, multicultural culinary platform featuring an AI Cooking Coach, intelligent recipe generation, and global flavor discovery. The project utilizes a "Modern Global Kitchen" aesthetic characterized by sophisticated typography, a warm color palette, and high-fidelity glassmorphism elements.
