Midnight Saffron &
Toasted Pine Nut Risotto

A sophisticated, slow-stirred classic optimized for focus and deep flavor profiles, perfect for quiet late-night sessions.

timer Total Time 45 Minutes
restaurant_menu Difficulty Intermediate
play_circle Guided Mode Start Cooking

Ingredients

  • check
    Arborio Rice
    350g
  • check
    Saffron Strands
    0.5g
  • check
    Vegetable Broth
    1.2L
  • check
    Pine Nuts
    50g
  • check
    Parmigiano Reggiano
    80g
  • check
    Shallots
    2 medium
lightbulb Pro Tip

Toast your pine nuts in a dry pan until golden. This enhances the oils and creates a sharp contrast with the creamy risotto.

Preparation

1

The Infusion

Warm the vegetable broth in a small pot. Take a ladle of warm broth and soak the saffron strands in it. Let it sit for at least 10 minutes to release the deep golden hue and aromatic compounds.

2

The Foundation

Sauté finely diced shallots in butter and a touch of olive oil over medium-low heat until translucent. Do not brown them. Add the Arborio rice and toast for 2 minutes until the edges are transparent.

3

The Ritual

Add the saffron infusion first. Then, ladle by ladle, add the warm broth. Stir constantly—this mechanical action releases the starch, creating the signature creaminess. Wait for the liquid to be absorbed before adding more.

psychology Kitchen Wisdom

"The secret to risotto is the stir. Never rush the rice; let the starch develop its own velvet texture."

4

The Mantecatura

Once the rice is al dente, remove from heat. Vigorously stir in cold butter cubes and freshly grated Parmigiano. Cover and let rest for 2 minutes. Fold in toasted pine nuts just before serving.
