Spiced Saffron & Avocado Harvest Bowl

schedule 25 min
local_fire_department 420 kcal
star 4.9 (124 reviews)

An elevated take on the classic harvest bowl. This recipe features our signature CulinaAI-generated saffron-infused dressing, perfectly balancing the creaminess of ripe Hass avocados with the earthy crunch of spiced chickpeas.

Ingredients

2 Servings

Step-by-step Instructions

01

Preparation

Rinse the quinoa under cold water. In a medium saucepan, combine quinoa with 400ml water and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer for 15 minutes.

02

Saffron Dressing

In a small glass bowl, bloom the saffron threads in 2 tablespoons of warm water for 5 minutes. Whisk in tahini, lemon juice, and a dash of maple syrup until silky smooth.

psychology

AI Pro Tip: If the dressing is too thick, add 1 tsp of warm water at a time until you reach a ribbon-like consistency.

03

Assembly

Massage the kale with a drop of olive oil. Layer the quinoa, chickpeas, and sliced avocado. Drizzle generously with the saffron dressing and top with sesame seeds.
